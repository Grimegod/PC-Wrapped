PC WRAPPED · PRIVACY

Your activity stays yours.

PC Wrapped is a Windows desktop app for local activity recaps. This page explains what the app stores, what it does not collect, and what happens when you choose to share a card.

What the app records

While tracking is on, PC Wrapped identifies the foreground application and measures active time. It stores daily app aggregates and recap-related counts on your PC. Activity history is retained for up to 365 days.

Tracking is paused until you start it. You can pause it at any time, exclude apps from future tracking, hide app names on cards, and delete all saved history from Settings.

What the app does not collect

PC Wrapped does not read window titles, browser URLs, document contents, screenshots, keyboard input, or clipboard contents. It does not require an account or send your activity history to a PC Wrapped server.

Sharing is your choice

Recap cards are generated and saved as PNG files on your PC. If you choose a sharing route, the app may open a provider with a short caption based on aggregate recap values, or copy the saved PNG to your clipboard. The app does not upload the image. You decide whether to attach or post it, and the external service’s privacy policy applies to anything you send there.

This presentation site

The site is static HTML and CSS. It does not include analytics, advertising pixels, embedded social feeds, or forms. It links to the Microsoft Store and GitHub; those services have their own privacy practices. The hosting provider may process standard request logs to deliver the page.

Questions or corrections

Use the project issue tracker to report a privacy question or request a correction.
