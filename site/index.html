GOOD TO KNOW

A few quick answers.

Does PC Wrapped judge how productive I am?

No. It summarizes active time by app and uses playful language for the recap. It does not measure attention, intent, or whether an activity was worthwhile.

What does it record?

When tracking is on, the app records the foreground app identity and active/idle timing, then stores daily aggregates. It does not read window titles or browser URLs.

Does my card get uploaded when I share?

No. The PNG is saved locally. If you pick a sharing route, PC Wrapped may open that service with a short caption or copy the PNG to your clipboard; you choose whether to attach or post anything.

Can I stop tracking or remove my history?

Yes. Tracking starts only after you choose it, can be paused from the app, and saved history can be deleted in Settings.
